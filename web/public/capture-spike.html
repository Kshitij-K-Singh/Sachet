  1. Chrome or Edge on desktop. Firefox and Safari cannot capture tab audio.
  2. In the picker choose Chrome Tab (not a window or the whole screen).
  3. Tick Share tab audio. Without it the capture is silent.

  5. Make sure the reel tab is actually playing with sound - many sites autoplay muted.
